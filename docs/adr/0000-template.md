# NNNN. Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](nnnn-title.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

The forces at play: the problem, the constraints, and what made this a real decision rather
than an obvious one. Written in the present tense of when the decision was made.

## Decision

The position taken, stated plainly. "We will …".

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we explicitly accept as a trade-off —
including the things we chose *not* to do.

## Alternatives considered

(Optional.) The options that were weighed and why they were rejected.
